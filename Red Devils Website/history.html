Our History

The story of the club, one moment at a time.

1878

The club is founded as Newton Heath LYR Football Club.

1902

The club officially becomes Manchester United.

1910

United move to their iconic home ground at Old Trafford.

1945

Matt Busby begins a legendary era, building a very young squad known as the "Busby Babes".

1958

Eight players lose their lives in the Munich air disaster. Busby rebuilds the team around George Best, Denis Law and Bobby Charlton.

1968

United become the first English club to win the European Cup.

1986

Alex Ferguson takes over as manager.

1999

The unprecedented treble: the Premier League, FA Cup and UEFA Champions League in one season (1998/99).

2013

Ferguson retires after 27 years and 38 trophies, including 13 league titles.

2017

José Mourinho wins the Europa League (2016/17), completing all three original flagship UEFA competitions.

2024

Sir Jim Ratcliffe (INEOS) buys a minority stake of nearly 28% and takes charge of football operations.

Rivalries

United have intense rivalries with Liverpool, Manchester City, Leeds United and Arsenal.

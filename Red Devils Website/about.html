About The Red Devils

A fan website dedicated to Manchester United, made by a supporter, for supporters.

Our Mission

We want to give fans one simple place to enjoy the story of the club. Whether you have followed United for fifty years or only just discovered them, you can learn about the history, see the trophies, meet the players and follow the biggest transfers.

Club Quick Facts

Fact Detail
Nickname The Red Devils
Founded 1878, as Newton Heath LYR Football Club
Became Manchester United 1902
Home ground Old Trafford, since 1910
Famous manager Sir Alex Ferguson (1986 to 2013)
Treble season 1998/99
Main rivals Liverpool, Manchester City, Leeds United, Arsenal
